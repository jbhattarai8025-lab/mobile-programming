Problem
Finding available parking spaces in busy areas of Nepal is difficult because there are limited parking facilities and no convenient way for drivers to know which spaces are available. Drivers often spend extra time searching for parking, which can cause traffic congestion, fuel consumption, and inconvenience. Parking operators also face difficulties managing spaces, bookings, and customer records manually.
Problem Statement
There is a need for a digital parking booking and management system that allows users to easily find available parking spaces, check prices and locations, and reserve a parking space in advance. The system should also help parking owners manage their parking spaces, bookings, and customer information efficiently.
Objectives
Main Objective:
 To develop a parking booking and management application that makes finding and reserving parking spaces easier and more efficient.
Specific Objectives:
To allow users to find nearby available parking spaces.
To provide information about parking location, price, and availability.
To allow users to book parking spaces in advance.
To provide booking confirmation through the application.
To maintain users' booking history and parking records.
To help parking owners manage spaces and bookings digitally.
To reduce the time and inconvenience involved in searching for parking.
